# Third-party components and preview artwork

This is a private, unsigned foundation preview. It does not grant permission to publish the application or to reuse third-party material outside its applicable terms.

- Electron and its bundled Chromium/Node components retain their supplied LICENSE and third-party notices.
- The runtime/build dependency inventory and copied license files are generated under `dist/notices` from the exact installed Node and Python distributions.
- The MediaPipe Face Landmarker model is pinned by `worker/resources/model-source.json`; the model card and source are tracked separately from code licenses.
- Premium preview textures were generated for this project and imported without modifying the phase04 originals. Source prompts and rights/use records remain in `experiments/phase04/assets/premium/generation-prompts.md`. They are a foundation sample, not an approved production parts library.
- OBS Studio and Inochi Session executables are not included in this product package. Experimental downloads remain development-only.
- Public distribution, signing, a full dependency/asset review and clean-OS validation remain release-gate work.
